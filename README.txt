MANSOOR ALI — DIGITAL MARKETING PORTFOLIO
==========================================

CONTENTS
- index.html   Main portfolio page
- style.css    Responsive styling and visual design
- script.js    Mobile navigation, reveal effects and dynamic year

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. For deployment, upload the extracted files to your hosting provider's public web directory.

IMPORTANT PERSONALIZATION BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Replace hello@yourdomain.com in index.html with your real business email (there are two occurrences).
2. Replace the LinkedIn and Instagram placeholder URLs with your profile links.
3. Add real client projects, screenshots, testimonials and verified outcomes when available.
   The work section currently describes capability examples and explicitly does not claim completed results.
4. Review the service list and brand wording so they accurately reflect your offer.

BRAND
The page presents Mansoor Ali as a digital marketer associated with Optiqo Growth Lab.
The site uses a dark premium visual style, responsive layouts, semantic HTML, accessible navigation
labels, reduced-motion support and no framework or build process.

NOTES
- Google Fonts are loaded online; system sans-serif fallbacks are provided.
- The contact CTA uses a mailto link. It opens the visitor's configured email application;
  it does not submit to a server or store enquiries.
